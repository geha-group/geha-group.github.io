    Nick completed his PhD at the Université libre de Bruxelles in 2026 and is now a Fulbright Postdoctoral Fellow at Yale University. His research focuses on astrophysical probes of dark matter. His previous work investigated the interactions between primordial black holes and stars in ultra-faint dwarf galaxies. He is now studying dark matter and stellar dynamics in these galaxies more broadly, from both theoretical and observational perspectives, with a particular focus on their binary star populations.
